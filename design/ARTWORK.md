# Guild hall artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, guided by the user's Horde army, banner bearer, and fortress references. The supplied reference files are not bundled in the application.

Source: `onslaught-warhost.png` (original generated image).
Web asset: `../public/art/onslaught-warhost.jpg` (same composition and dimensions, JPEG quality 88 for loading speed).

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide hero artwork for Ønslaught, a World of Warcraft Forever Horde guild headquarters website. Create original polished painterly fantasy illustration inspired by the supplied visual direction: a powerful green-skinned orc champion in battered dark iron and crimson spiked armor, carrying a heavy axe, before a towering Horde fortress with red war banners, canyon cliffs, torch fire, distant gathered warriors and a few wyverns in a smoky dusk sky. Classic Warcraft concept-art energy, rich brushwork, sharp readable silhouette, cinematic depth, sophisticated detail rather than glossy photorealism. Composition: panoramic landscape, roughly 2.5:1 ratio; champion bust and axe occupy the RIGHT THIRD, face near upper right, fortress and banners recede through the middle; the LEFT 45 percent is quieter dark charcoal smoky sky and shadowed canyon to leave room for large interface heading text. Keep important face and banners within middle 75 percent of height so this crops well into a wide header. Lighting: fierce ember-orange rim light and cool steel reflections; saturated crimson cloth and rich green skin against near-black charcoal shadows. No sepia wash, no fog washing out all contrast. Heroic and resolute, no gore. No lettering, captions, UI frames, artist signature or watermark. This is an illustration asset, not a website mockup.

## Interface direction

Charcoal panels, saturated crimson actions, readable cool-white body text, silver Goodmountain display lettering, and slim, visible metal borders. The illustrated header carries the decoration; navigation, forms, raid groups and chat keep restrained surfaces. WoW class and readiness colors retain their meanings.
